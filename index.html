About FixMySales

Sales support for software companies, built around your pipeline.

FixMySales is a software sales and business development practice founded by Aniket Gadre, focused on helping SaaS and software companies create new B2B sales opportunities. The approach combines prospect research, lead generation, outbound sales, business development, sales conversations, qualification, follow-up, negotiation and closing support into a single, structured sales motion.

Rather than treating sales as a single activity, FixMySales works across the full cycle — from understanding a product and its ideal customer, through to supporting the final stages of a deal — so software companies can spend more time building product and less time chasing pipeline.
